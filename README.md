# Enterprise AI Adoption and Subscription Analysis

A group business analytics project examining how employees use ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot and how an organization can improve AI subscription allocation, adoption, and governance.

## Business questions

- Which AI tool has broader adoption and stronger habitual use?
- Are paid subscriptions aligned with actual employee usage?
- How does usage differ by job category, employment type, and managerial status?
- Where might the organization face resource waste, access gaps, or governance risks?

## Analysis workflow

1. Integrated employee, subscription, ChatGPT usage, and Copilot usage records.
2. Cleaned identifiers, activity fields, employment categories, and usage measures.
3. Created comparable metrics using usage per active day rather than raw totals alone.
4. Classified Copilot users into super-active, high-frequency, low-frequency, and inactive tiers.
5. Compared adoption and usage across roles, worker types, and management status.
6. Converted the findings into management recommendations and an executive report.

## Selected findings

- Among 105 employees, 76 had used ChatGPT and 72 had used Copilot.
- Forty-eight employees used both tools, representing approximately 46% of the workforce.
- After adjusting for active days, 58 users used Copilot more frequently and 28 used ChatGPT more frequently.
- Producer roles had high Copilot subscription coverage but substantially lower usage than other roles.
- Contingent workers showed usage intensity close to full-time employees but had very limited official subscription coverage.
- Managers and non-managers showed little difference in usage intensity.
- Approximately 20%-30% of subscribed accounts showed no usage, indicating potential license waste.

## Business recommendations

- Reallocate licenses using observed activity and role-specific needs.
- Provide compliant access pathways for contingent workers.
- Introduce scenario-based AI training for Producers and managers.
- Review dormant subscriptions regularly and establish adoption KPIs.
